# Easy Plan + Meals

The simple version of [BRAIN-STAMINA-PLAN.md](BRAIN-STAMINA-PLAN.md): cook twice a week,
eat the same few meals, train 5 days. Based on ~75 kg (165 lb) → **~120 g protein, ~2,200 kcal/day**.
Bigger or smaller? Scale the portions up or down by about 10%.

## 3. Recipes (all easy)

### A. Masala oats with eggs (10 min, 1 serving)
- ½ cup oats · ½ onion · ½ bell pepper · ½ tomato (all chopped) · ¼ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp cumin · salt · 1 cup water
- Sauté the cumin, onion and pepper in 1 tsp oil for 2 min → add the tomato, spices and oats → add the water and cook 3–4 min.
- On the side: **2 eggs + 2 egg whites** (bhurji or boiled).

### B. Overnight protein oats (2 min the night before, 1 serving)
- ½ cup oats · 1 scoop whey · ¾ cup milk · 1 tbsp chia · ½ cup berries or banana · cinnamon
- Mix in a jar and refrigerate. Eat cold. **Make 3 jars at once.**

### C. Sheet-pan chicken tikka + peppers (35 min, 5 servings)
- 1 kg (2.2 lb) chicken breast or thigh, cut into cubes
- Marinade: 1 cup curd/Greek yogurt · 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste · 2 tsp Kashmiri chili · 1 tsp turmeric · 2 tsp garam masala · 1 tsp cumin · 1½ tsp salt · juice of 1 lemon · 1 tbsp oil
- 3 bell peppers + 2 onions, cut into chunks
- Marinate 30 min (or overnight) → spread on a tray with the peppers and onions → bake at **220 °C / 425 °F for 20–25 min** → broil 2 min for char.

### D. Masala sweet potato (30 min, 5 servings)
- 4 medium sweet potatoes, cubed (skin on) · 1 tbsp oil · 1 tsp cumin · 1 tsp chaat masala · ½ tsp chili · salt
- Toss everything → roast **220 °C / 425 °F for 25 min** (same oven as the chicken, on a second tray).

### E. Dal tadka (25 min, 5 servings, Instant Pot or pressure cooker)
- 1½ cups masoor or moong dal (rinsed) · 4½ cups water · ½ tsp turmeric · salt → pressure cook **8 min** (3 whistles)
- Tadka: 1 tbsp ghee · 1 tsp cumin · 4 garlic cloves · 1 onion · 1 tomato · 1 green chili · ½ tsp garam masala → pour onto the dal
- Stir in 2 handfuls of spinach at the end (iron + immunity).

### F. Chana or rajma (Prep #2 option, 5 servings)
- Same method as the dal, using 2 cans of chickpeas or kidney beans (no pressure cooking needed): tadka → add the beans + 1 cup water → simmer 10 min.

### G. Rice / roti
- Brown or basmati rice: cook 3 cups dry for the week. Or 2 whole-wheat roti per meal.

## 4. Prep schedule

**Prep #1, Sunday (~90 min). Covers Mon–Wed:**
1. Marinate the chicken (C) → start the dal (E) in the cooker
2. Oven: chicken + sweet potato (C + D) together
3. Cook the rice
4. Make 3 overnight oats jars (B)
5. Pack 3 lunch boxes: chicken + peppers + sweet potato

**Prep #2, Wednesday (~45 min). Covers Thu–Fri:**
1. Chicken + sweet potato again (C + D), or swap in paneer tikka for variety
2. Chana or rajma (F) for dinners

Fridge: cooked chicken and dal keep **4 days**. Freeze any extra portions.

## 6. Supplements (keep it simple)

Daily: **creatine 5 g** (any time) · **vitamin D3 2,000 IU** (with food) · **omega-3 1 g** · **magnesium glycinate 300 mg** (at bedtime).
Get blood work first (Vit D, B12, iron, HbA1c, lipids), especially B12 if you eat mostly dal and grains.

## 7. Better practices

- **Same breakfast and lunch every weekday.** Fewer decisions makes it easier to stick with.
- **Prep on fixed days** (Sun + Wed), and put them on your calendar.
- **Protein at every meal.** If a meal falls short, add curd, eggs or whey.
- **Weigh yourself weekly** (same day, morning). Losing more than 0.5 kg/week or no strength gains → eat more rice or sweet potato.
- **No sugar drinks, no alcohol on weeknights, no caffeine after 2 pm.**
- **Track in Karma:** protein ✓ · workout ✓ · steps ✓ · bed on time ✓.
