## 3. Weekly training split (~45–60 min/session)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | **Strength A** — Squat, Bench/Push-up, Row, Plank |
| Tue | **Zone 2** 40 min (brisk walk incline / bike / jog — can still talk in full sentences) |
| Wed | **Strength B** — Deadlift/RDL, Overhead press, Pull-up/Lat pulldown, Lunges |
| Thu | **Zone 2** 40 min + 10 min mobility |
| Fri | **Strength C** — Goblet squat, Incline press, 1-arm row, Farmer carry |
| Sat | **VO₂ max intervals** — 4 × 4 min hard / 3 min easy (from week 3) + Sport/fun |
| Sun | Rest: long walk, stretching, sauna if available |

**Strength rules:** 3 sets × 6–12 reps, last 1–2 reps hard (RPE 8). Add weight or 1 rep each
week (progressive overload). Log every session.

**Readiness rule (Oura/Vitara):** readiness < 70 or HRV well below baseline → swap hard day
for Zone 2 or rest. Sick above the neck = light walk only; below the neck/fever = full rest.

## 4. 12-week progression

| Weeks | Focus | Cardio | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Habit + form | Zone 2 2 × 30 min | 2 sets, light, learn technique |
| 3–6 | Build base | Zone 2 2 × 40 min + 1 interval day | 3 sets, add load weekly |
| 7 | **Deload** | Easy only | 50% volume |
| 8–11 | Push | Zone 2 2 × 45 min + 4×4 intervals | 3–4 sets, heavier (6–8 reps) |
| 12 | Deload + retest | Easy | Retest benchmarks |

## 5. Nutrition (simple template)

- **Plate:** ½ veg, ¼ protein, ¼ complex carbs (rice/oats/millets/quinoa), thumb of fat.
- **Protein sources:** eggs, Greek yogurt/curd, paneer, dal + rice, chicken, fish, tofu, whey.
- **Brain foods:** fatty fish 2×/week (or algae omega-3), walnuts, berries, leafy greens, eggs.
- **Immunity/gut:** curd/kefir/idli/dosa batter (fermented), garlic, ginger, turmeric + pepper,
  citrus, legumes, 30 g fiber/day.
- **Limit:** ultra-processed food, added sugar, alcohol (hurts sleep, HRV, muscle and immunity).
- **Caffeine:** none after 1–2 pm.

**Supplements (evidence-backed; check blood work first):**
| Supplement | Dose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vitamin D3 (+K2) | 1,000–2,000 IU (per blood level) | immunity, muscle |
| Creatine monohydrate | 3–5 g daily | muscle, strength, brain energy |
| Omega-3 (EPA+DHA) | 1–2 g | brain, inflammation |
| Magnesium glycinate | 200–400 mg at night | sleep |
| Whey/plant protein | as needed to hit target | muscle |

## 6. Brain-specific stack

1. **Exercise** is the #1 brain booster — the training above already covers it.
2. **Learn something hard** (language, instrument, chess, coding a new domain) — 20 min/day.
3. **Active recall + spaced repetition** (Anki) instead of re-reading.
4. **Single-tasking:** 90-min focus blocks, 15-min breaks; notifications off.
5. **Meditation:** 10 min/day (attention + stress).
6. **Social:** meaningful conversation daily — strong predictor of long-term cognition.
7. **Reading:** 20 pages/day, books > feeds.

## 7. Measure it (baseline Week 0 → retest Week 6 & 12)

| Area | Test |
|---|---|
| Stamina | 1.5-mile run time or 12-min Cooper test; resting HR (Oura) |
| Muscle | Max push-ups, dead hang time, 5-rep squat weight; waist + weight |
| Recovery | 7-day avg HRV, readiness, sleep score (Vitara) |
| Brain | Dual N-Back level or reaction-time test; subjective focus 1–10 |
| Immunity | Sick days/quarter; blood: CBC, Vit D, B12, ferritin, HbA1c, lipids, TSH |

## 8. Better practices

- **Track in Maaya:** put the 10 habits into Karma; let Vitara drive the readiness rule.
- **Never miss twice:** missing one day is fine; never two in a row.
- **Schedule workouts** as calendar blocks, same time daily.
- **Start smaller than you think** — consistency beats intensity in weeks 1–4.
- **Blood work every 6 months** to tune supplements instead of guessing.
